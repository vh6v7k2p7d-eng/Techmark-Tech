Problem solved

Stop running property sales through scattered files and missed follow-ups.

Many real estate teams depend on WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets, calls and scattered document folders. That makes inventory hard to track, follow-ups easy to miss and team performance difficult to measure.

  • Centralize property inventory, availability and media.
  • Track every lead from first inquiry to site visit and closure.

Core features

Everything your real estate team needs to organize leads and inventory.

The portal can be deployed as a ready system and customized with modules based on your workflow.

Property Inventory

Manage listings, images, documents, property status, unit details and availability from one place.

Lead Management

Capture, assign and track leads across follow-up stages, inquiries, site visits and proposals.

Role-Based Access

Create admin, broker, sales team and manager roles with controlled access to relevant data.

Inquiry Dashboard

View inquiry source, response status, follow-up reminders and customer journey history.

Reports and Analytics

Track lead volume, source quality, deal status, team activity and property performance.

Custom Modules

Add WhatsApp integration, payment status, CRM fields, document workflows or custom reports.
